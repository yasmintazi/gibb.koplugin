# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-14)

First version.

- Dashboard with Home, Shelf, Notes and Stats pages
- Book pages with status, star rating, review, progress and reading stats
- Rate and review prompt at the end of a book
- Library with grid and list views, search, filters and sorting
- List of all highlights across books
- Reader menu, swipe-down quick panel, header and footer stats
- Auto-highlight for long selections
- App tiles for Z-library, AO3 Downloader, Storefront and Crossword
- Pauses its in-book features while Aleph is on, with a switch to turn Aleph off
